snapshot is `lib/` from commit `97bd336` with one change: `thinkingBudget: 0`
replaced by `thinkingLevel: "minimal"`, because `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` rejects
the former with a 400. Model: `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` (`GEMINI_MODEL`), the same

the single-Gemini path.
